## What it answers

The contracts of __core__ this provider implements, and what it does not (each refused with the
family's "not supported" exception).

## Errors

The family's rule (see __core__'s docs): the service's refusals and unreadable answers are its
provider exception, a request that got no answer its unreachable one.

## Usage policy

`policy.json`, at the package's root, is __Title__'s usage policy as data (omnisense/contracts'
schema): its rate limits, its rights, what a request must carry, the terms it was read from and
when. Read the terms at their source, fill it in, and keep `provenance.verifiedAt` the day it was
last checked. A site may tighten it in its configuration (`policy`), never loosen it.
